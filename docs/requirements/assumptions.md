# Assumptions

- The initial audience is prospective clients evaluating AST for software/AI
  work, not end consumers.
- English-only content for v1.
- No payment processing in v1.
- Hosting/deployment target is a standard Node-compatible platform (see
  `../deployment/environments.md`) — final provider choice is TBD.

confirmed business facts. Revisit before major decisions.
